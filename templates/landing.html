How to Apply for a Stall

Standard procedure for leasing a public market stall.

1

Apply at the Market Office

Visit the {{ system_settings.office_name|default:"Market Office" }} and submit your application form and required identification.

2

Sign the Rental Contract

Upon approval, execute the rental contract covering stall assignment, rates, due dates, and security deposit.

3

Pay Monthly Rent

Settle monthly rent at the cashier or authorized collector on or before the due date and keep your official receipt.
