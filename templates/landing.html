Online Services

Services provided by the {{ system_settings.office_name|default:"Market Office" }} through this system.

Stall Rentals

Billing & Collections

How to Apply for a Stall

Procedure for renting a public market stall.

1

Apply at the Market Office

Visit the {{ system_settings.office_name|default:"Market Office" }} and submit your application form and requirements.

2

Sign the Rental Contract

Once approved, sign the rental contract covering stall assignment, rates, due dates, and deposit.

3

Pay Monthly Rent

Pay at the cashier or authorized collector on or before the due date. Always keep your official receipt.
